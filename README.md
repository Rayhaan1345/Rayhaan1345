### Rayhaan Khan

A Level student in Navi Mumbai working on machine learning research. Research intern at [MBZUAI](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/) and founder of [Mumbai AI Lab](https://mailab.in).

Website: [rayhaan1345.github.io](https://rayhaan1345.github.io)

**Research**

- [inplace-ttt-study](https://github.com/Rayhaan1345/inplace-ttt-study): a controlled ablation of In-Place Test-Time Training on Qwen3-4B. Switching the test-time update off changes the RULER score by at most 0.4 points up to 64k tokens, so the gains come from training with the module.
- *Stable Continual Pre-training from Weights-Only Checkpoints via Rebuilt Optimizer State*, with Shadab Khan. Under review at TMLR. [Manuscript](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VI6MjFcu0oSlswk5wuyVrYFZPcKXPKhP/view)
- [Diabetes-Predictor-Model](https://github.com/Rayhaan1345/Diabetes-Predictor-Model): diabetes risk screening from routine measurements, built at M42 in 2024 and organised with Microsoft's Team Data Science Process. [Demo app](https://diabetes-predictor-model.streamlit.app)
